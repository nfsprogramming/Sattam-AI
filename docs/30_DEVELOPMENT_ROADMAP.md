# ⚖️ SattamAI Development Roadmap

**Version:** 1.0

**Project:** SattamAI – AI-Powered Legal Assistant

**Estimated Duration:** 12 Months

---

# Overview

This roadmap defines the complete execution plan for building, testing, deploying, and maintaining the SattamAI platform. It includes development phases, milestones, deliverables, team responsibilities, and future expansion plans.

The roadmap follows an incremental approach, beginning with a Minimum Viable Product (MVP) and expanding into a nationwide AI-powered legal assistance platform.

---

# Vision

To make Indian laws understandable and accessible by providing AI-assisted legal information, multilingual support, document generation, government department guidance, and lawyer connectivity through a secure mobile application.

---

```text
Month 1–2   : Planning & Research
Month 3–4   : Core Backend & Mobile Development
Month 5–6   : AI Integration & RAG Development

Month 12    : Production Launch
```
