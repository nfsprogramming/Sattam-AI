# ⚖️ SattamAI – AI-Powered Legal Assistant for India

## Overview

SattamAI is an AI-powered legal assistant designed to help people understand Indian laws in simple language. It enables users to describe legal issues using text, voice, or images and receive structured guidance based on applicable laws, legal procedures, required documents, and available legal services.

The long-term vision is to build a specialized AI model trained on Indian legal knowledge, making legal information more accessible, understandable, and actionable for everyone.

---

# Vision

To make Indian law understandable and accessible for every citizen through artificial intelligence while providing reliable legal information, procedural guidance, and document assistance.

---

# Mission

Develop an AI-first legal platform that helps users:

- Understand applicable laws.
- Learn their legal rights and responsibilities.
- Identify the correct government department.
- Prepare the required legal documents.
- Navigate legal procedures.
- Connect with legal professionals when appropriate.

---

# Problem Statement

Many citizens struggle to understand legal procedures due to:

- Complex legal terminology.
- Lack of awareness of legal rights.
- Difficulty identifying the correct government department.
- Limited access to legal guidance.
- Language barriers.
- Uncertainty about required documents and procedures.

SattamAI addresses these challenges by providing AI-powered legal guidance in simple Tamil and English.

# AI Model

SattamAI will be developed using a specialized legal AI model based on an open-source large language model.

The model will be adapted for Indian legal knowledge and supported by a retrieval system for up-to-date legal information.

Primary capabilities include:

- Legal reasoning
- Law identification
- Department identification
- Document generation
- Legal procedure guidance
- Bilingual responses (Tamil and English)
