# ⚖️ SattamAI Legal Model Card

**Model Name:** SattamAI Legal Model

**Version:** 0.1 (Prototype)

**Status:** Under Development

**Project:** SattamAI – AI-Powered Legal Assistant for India

---

# Overview

The SattamAI Legal Model is a specialized Large Language Model (LLM) designed to assist users in understanding Indian laws, legal procedures, government departments, and required documentation.

The model is developed by adapting an open-source foundation model and combining it with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to provide grounded, explainable, and structured legal guidance.

Its primary objective is to simplify legal information while encouraging users to consult qualified legal professionals for matters requiring legal representation or advice.

---

# Intended Use

The model is intended for:

- Explaining Indian laws in simple language.
- Identifying applicable legal domains.
- Explaining legal rights and responsibilities.
- Suggesting common procedural next steps.
- Identifying relevant government departments.
- Listing commonly required documents.
- Drafting common legal document templates.
- Supporting legal education and awareness.

---

# Out of Scope

The model is **not** intended to:

- Replace licensed lawyers.
- Provide legal representation.
- Predict court outcomes.
- Guarantee legal success.
- Create legally binding advice.
- Make judicial or administrative decisions.

When appropriate, the model should clearly indicate that professional legal assistance may be necessary.

# AI Capabilities

The model is expected to support:

- Legal explanation.

- Multi-turn conversations.

---

# Limitations

The model has important limitations:

- It may not have access to the latest legal developments unless the knowledge base is updated.
- It cannot replace professional legal judgment.
- It may require additional context for complex legal matters.
- It should not be relied upon as the sole source for high-stakes legal decisions.
- It may decline to answer questions outside its intended legal scope.

---

# Safety Principles

The model should:

- Distinguish legal information from legal advice.
- Base responses on retrieved legal information whenever possible.
- Avoid unsupported legal conclusions.
- Acknowledge uncertainty when appropriate.
- Encourage users to seek qualified legal assistance for complex or high-risk situations.

---

# Performance Goals

The model should aim to provide:

- Clear explanations.
- Consistent response structure.
- High retrieval relevance.
- Accurate legal domain identification.
- Good multilingual quality.
- Responsive user experience.

Performance should be evaluated continuously as the project evolves.

---

# Privacy Considerations

The model should be integrated into systems that:

- Protect user data.
- Handle uploaded documents securely.
- Limit data access to authorized processes.
- Follow applicable privacy and data protection requirements.

Users should avoid sharing unnecessary personal or sensitive information.

# Responsible Use

SattamAI is designed to improve access to legal information and procedural guidance. It should be used as an educational and informational tool that complements, rather than replaces, professional legal services.

Users are encouraged to verify important legal information and consult qualified legal professionals when their circumstances require formal legal advice or representation.

---

# Summary

The SattamAI Legal Model combines a specialized language model with a maintained legal knowledge base to provide clear, explainable, and responsible legal guidance. Its design prioritizes accessibility, transparency, and continuous improvement while recognizing the limits of AI in legal decision-making.
