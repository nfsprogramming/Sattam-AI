# ⚖️ SattamAI Feature Specification

## Introduction

SattamAI is designed as a comprehensive AI-powered legal platform that helps users understand laws, identify the correct authorities, prepare legal documents, and navigate legal procedures. This document defines the functional features planned for the platform.

---

# 1. AI Legal Assistant

The AI Legal Assistant is the primary feature of SattamAI.

Users can interact with the AI using:

- Text
- Voice
- Images
- Uploaded documents

The AI analyzes the user's legal situation and provides structured guidance.

### Output Structure

- Problem Summary
- Applicable Legal Domain
- Relevant Laws
- Rights
- Responsibilities
- Required Documents
- Government Department
- Step-by-Step Procedure
- Suggested Next Actions
- Disclaimer (when professional legal advice may be required)

---

# 2. Legal Situation Analysis

The AI analyzes user input to determine:

- Type of legal issue
- Applicable legal domain
- Severity level
- Possible authorities involved
- General procedural guidance

Example categories include:

- Traffic
- Cyber Crime
- Consumer
- Labour
- Property
- Family
- Banking
- Tax
- Civil
- Criminal

---

# 3. Department Identification

Automatically identify the most appropriate authority based on the user's issue.

Examples:

- Police Station
- Traffic Police
- Cyber Crime Police
- Labour Department
- Revenue Department
- Registration Department
- Municipality
- Transport Department
- Consumer Commission
- Passport Office
- Electricity Board
- Water Board
- Forest Department

---

# 4. Legal Knowledge Search

Search across a maintained legal knowledge base containing:

- Constitution
- Central Acts
- State Acts
- Rules
- Regulations
- Government Orders
- Notifications
- Circulars
- Official procedures

The AI presents relevant information in simple language while referencing the applicable legal sources where available.

---

# 5. Rights and Responsibilities

For every legal issue, SattamAI explains:

- Legal rights
- Legal responsibilities
- Obligations
- Available remedies
- Practical considerations

---

# 6. Required Documents

Display documents commonly required for the identified process.

Examples include:

- Identity proof
- Address proof
- Driving Licence
- Registration Certificate (RC)
- Insurance documents
- Property records
- Employment records

---

# 7. Step-by-Step Legal Guidance

Provide procedural guidance such as:

- Where to go
- Whom to contact
- What to carry
- What to expect
- Typical next steps

---

# 8. AI Document Generator

Generate editable drafts for:

- Complaint letters
- Legal notices
- Applications
- Representation letters
- Request letters

Users can:

- Edit
- Save
- Export
- Share

---


Extract text from uploaded:

- FIR copies

# 10. Voice Assistant

Support:

- Voice input
- Speech-to-text
- Voice responses
- Hands-free interaction

---

# 11. Lawyer Directory

Allow users to:

- Search nearby lawyers
- Filter by legal specialization
- View ratings (where available)
- Contact lawyers
- Request appointments

---

# 12. Case Management

Users can:

- Save cases
- Track progress
- Organize documents
- View timelines
- Manage legal history

---

# 13. User Profile

Store:

- Personal preferences
- Language selection
- Saved cases
- Saved documents
- Notifications

---

# 14. Multilingual Support

Primary languages:

- Tamil
- English

Future releases may include additional Indian languages.

---

# 15. Search by Legal Topic

Users can browse information by category such as:

- Traffic
- Cyber Crime
- Labour
- Consumer

- Government Services

# 16. Emergency Guidance

For situations that may require immediate attention, the platform should prioritize urgent procedural information and clearly indicate when emergency services or qualified legal assistance should be contacted.

Examples include:

- Domestic violence
- Missing persons
- Child safety concerns
- Serious cyber fraud
- Public safety emergencies

---

# 17. Notifications

Notify users about:

- Saved case updates
- Document reminders
- Upcoming appointments
- Platform announcements

---

# 18. Accessibility

Support:

- Large buttons
- Clear typography
- Voice interaction
- Simple navigation
- Easy-to-understand language
